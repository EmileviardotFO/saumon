Take-out rate × implied growth × price — index base 100 = historical norm for that calendar month
Each series is rebased so that 100 is the average value of that same calendar month across the whole history — every June against the mean of all Junes. That strips out seasonality and puts three different units on one scale. It does not compare N to N−1: that comparison is the vertical gap between the solid curve and the N−1 curve of the same colour. Take-out above 100 while implied growth sits at or below 100 is the borrowing-from-stock configuration.
The price curve is national and does not respond to the zone filter — no price exists by production area. It is the Akvafakta monthly export value ÷ export volume, a descriptive context curve: testing showed price tracks contemporaneous harvest volume (r = −0.66 on year-on-year changes) with no usable lead from the supply ratios, so do not read it as a forecast. Gaps in the line are months with no report (May–November 2023 in particular).

Calibre and premium, one line per year
Harvested average weight and volume come from the Biomasseregisteret. Calibre shares and premiums come from the weekly Akvafakta split (NASDAQ index up to week 18 of 2025, Sitagri after). Shares appear twice — by headcount as published, and by tonnage, which is what actually reaches the market and is always the larger figure. Premiums are the volume-weighted price of the band minus the 3–4 kg price. This tab is national and ignores the zone filter — no calibre or price data exists by production area.

Cumulative by year adds up a chosen month range and shows one line per year, so you can read a season across the whole series rather than one pair at a time. Exports by market
Volume, value and realised price for the four markets Akvafakta reports monthly: China and Hong Kong, the United States, the EU27 and Japan. Everything below is rebuilt from the monthly reports each time one is added, so this tab follows the data with no manual step. Volumes are round weight, values are the declared export value, so the implied price mixes product formats — a market buying fillet shows a higher price per kilo than one buying whole fish. Compare movements between periods, not levels between countries.

Product per market
Norwegian salmon exports by destination and by product, from Statistics Norway (table 08799, monthly). Volumes are product weight — a kilo of fillet counts as one kilo, as it crosses the border — which is what makes this tab a complement to Akvafakta's round-weight figures in the Markets tab rather than a duplicate: the gap between the two tells you how processed each market's purchases are. Values are FOB, in Norwegian kroner. Recent months are provisional and are revised by SSB for up to two years; the note at the bottom says how many of the months shown are. Mortality rate: cumulative mortality over the period, compounded month by month from the real monthly rates (dead/stock for each month) — not an average or a simple sum of the monthly rates. The national total compounds a national monthly rate the same way, weighted by each PO's actual headcount, month by month (not an average of the 13 zone rates). Live stock count: number of fish alive at the end of the last month of the period (not a cumulative total). Average harvest weight: total harvested weight ÷ number harvested, cumulated over the period. Average time at sea: weighted average (by number of fish harvested) of the number of months elapsed between each cohort's release and its harvest — the release month for each cohort is itself a weighted average by PO (not at the individual farm level); for cohorts released before 2017 (outside the available history), a default release month (June) is assumed. Global smolt released: all fish released into the sea under 500 g, cumulated over the period, split into < 250 g (UTSETT_SMOLT_STK) and 250–500 g (UTSETT_SMOLT_STK_MINDRE_ENN_500G minus UTSETT_SMOLT_STK — the 500 g column already contains the < 250 g fish). Fish released above 500 g are in neither column.
Estimated post-smolt > 500 g: fish that entered the water according to the stock count (closing stock − opening stock + harvest + mortality + other losses, per zone and month) minus Global smolt released. Fish released above 500 g are in no column of the source, so this gap is the only way to see them. It is an estimate, ±5–8 M fish a year: 2018–2023 hovered between −8 and +8 M (counting errors, reporting lags), then jumped to about +25 M a year in 2024–2025, concentrated in zones 2, 3 and 4 (south-west). Transfers between zones cancel out at national level (about 1–2 M of bias). Read it on a full year, never month by month, and ignore 2022 (December 2022 releases are over-declared by ~50 M).
Average stock weight: stock biomass ÷ headcount, at the end of the last month of the period (snapshot, like "Live stock count").
Cohort growth trend (kg/month): calculated cohort by cohort (release year), not on the PO's raw average weight — insensitive to new smolt and to the month's harvests. Value = average of the monthly weight changes over the selected period (averaged across cohorts present each month, weighted by headcount; the national total/national view additionally weights by each PO's headcount). For a finer cohort-by-cohort analysis (not blended by PO), see the "Growth by cohort" tab. Two known limitations: a slightly negative age at the very start of a cohort is an artifact of the average release month; an apparently negative growth at the very end of a cohort's life (very small residual headcount) generally reflects a selection effect at harvest — the largest fish leave first — not an actual biological shrinkage.
Feed consumption: cumulated over the period, in tonnes.
Smolt weight at release: only the size band is known (< 250 g, 250–500 g). The source gives no weight or biomass at release, and nothing about fish released above 500 g.
Source: Fiskeridirektoratet, Biomasseregisteret.
